Amlodipine + Losartan

Categories: Cardiovascular & Blood Pressure

Price:

  • Amlodipine + Losartan 5mg/50mg₱ 18

Description:

This medication is a combination of a calcium channel blocker (Amlodipine) and an angiotensin II receptor blocker (Losartan). Together, they provide a dual-action mechanism to relax blood vessels and lower blood pressure more effectively than either drug alone.

Uses:

  • Treatment of moderate to severe hypertension
  • Management of high blood pressure not controlled by a single medication
  • Reduction of cardiovascular risks in hypertensive patients
