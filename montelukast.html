Description:

Montelukast is a leukotriene receptor antagonist. It blocks leukotrienes inflammatory chemicals released by the body in response to allergens—which helps to reduce swelling and mucus production in the airways.

Uses:

  • Long-term maintenance and prophylaxis of asthma
  • Prevention of exercise-induced bronchospasm
  • Relief of symptoms of seasonal allergic rhinitis
