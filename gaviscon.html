Description:

Gaviscon is an antacid containing sodium alginate. Upon contact with stomach acid, the alginate forms a protective, buoyant foam barrier (a "raft") that floats on top of the stomach contents, preventing acid from refluxing back into the esophagus.

Uses:

  • Fast relief of acid reflux (GERD)
  • Management of severe heartburn
  • Relief of acid indigestion and dyspepsia
