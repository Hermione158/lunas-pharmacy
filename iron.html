Description:

These are hematinic supplements that combine elemental iron with folic acid and often other vitamins. They are essential for the production of hemoglobin in red blood cells and for proper cellular division.

Uses:

  • Prevention and treatment of iron-deficiency anemia
  • Nutritional support during pregnancy and lactation
  • Prevention of neural tube defects in developing fetuses
  • Treatment of severe blood loss
