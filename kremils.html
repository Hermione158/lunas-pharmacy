Kremil-S Antacid
(Kremil-S, Kremil-S Advance)

Categories: Gastrointestinal & Digestive Health

Price:

  • Kremil-S 178mg/233mg/30mg₱ 12
  • Kremil-S Advance 10mg/800mg/165mg₱ 27

Description:

Kremil-S is a combination medication containing acid-neutralizing compounds (aluminum and magnesium hydroxide) and an anti-flatulent (simethicone). It neutralizes excess stomach acid while breaking up trapped gas bubbles in the gut.

Uses:

  • Relief of hyperacidity and stomach ulcers
  • Relief of trapped stomach gas and bloating
  • Management of acid indigestion
