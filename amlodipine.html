Amlodipine

Categories: Cardiovascular & Blood Pressure

Price:

  • Amlodipine 5 mg₱ 6

Description:

Amlodipine is a calcium channel blocker that works by relaxing the blood vessel walls, allowing blood to flow more easily throughout the body. This reduces the workload on the heart and improves overall blood circulation.

Uses:

  • Management of hypertension (high blood pressure)
  • Relief of chronic stable angina (chest pain)
  • Cardiovascular maintenance therapy
