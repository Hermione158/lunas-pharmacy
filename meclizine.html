Meclizine HCl
(Bonamine, Meclizsaph)

Categories: Anti-Vertigo & Motion Sickness

  • Meclizsaph (Meclizine HCl) 25 mg₱ 12

Description:

Meclizine is an antihistamine with strong anticholinergic properties. It works by depressing the vestibular apparatus of the inner ear and blocking signals to the vomiting center of the brain.

Uses:

  • Prevention and treatment of motion sickness (travel nausea)
  • Management of vertigo associated with diseases affecting the vestibular system
  • Relief of dizziness
