Muscle Relaxant Combinations
(Norgesic, Norgesic Forte)

Categories: Pain Relief & Anti-Inflammatory (Analgesic / NSAID)

Price:

  • Norgesic₱ 27
  • Norgesic Forte₱ 45

Description:

This medication combines a standard analgesic (paracetamol) with a centrally acting skeletal muscle relaxant (orphenadrine citrate). It works by blocking pain sensations and relaxing muscle tension simultaneously.

Uses:

  • Relief of painful muscle spasms
  • Management of acute lower back pain
  • Treatment of sprains and strains
  • Relief of tension headaches
