Bisacodyl Laxative
(Dulcolax, Dylax)

Categories: Gastrointestinal & Digestive Health

Price:

  • Dylax (Bisacodyl) 5 mg₱ 15

Description:

Bisacodyl is a stimulant laxative. It works by directly stimulating the nerve endings in the wall of the large intestine, promoting peristalsis and increasing the secretion of water and electrolytes into the bowel to help relieve constipation.

Uses:

  • Short-term relief of occasional constipation
  • Bowel preparation before diagnostic procedures
  • Management of constipation associated with slow colonic transit
