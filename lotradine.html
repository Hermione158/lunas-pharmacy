Description:

Loratadine is a long-acting, non-drowsy antihistamine. It provides 24-hour relief from allergy symptoms by selectively blocking peripheral histamine receptors without crossing the blood-brain barrier.

Uses:

  • 24-hour relief of seasonal and perennial allergic rhinitis
  • Treatment of chronic idiopathic urticaria (hives)
  • Relief of allergic skin reactions
