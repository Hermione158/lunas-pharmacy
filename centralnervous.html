Central Nervous System
(Biperiden HCl)

Categories: Other Specialty & Oral Care

Price:

  • Biperiden HCl 2 mg₱ 26

Description:

Biperiden is an anticholinergic medication that acts on the central nervous system. It works by blocking the action of acetylcholine, thereby restoring the balance of neurotransmitters in the brain's motor centers.

Uses:

  • Treatment of Parkinson's disease symptoms
  • Management of drug-induced extrapyramidal symptoms (tremors, stiffness)
  • Relief of severe muscle rigidity
