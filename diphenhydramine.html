Diphenhydramine
(Mucomed, Histazyn)

Categories: Cough, Cold, Allergy & Respiratory

Price:

  • Mucomed (Diphenhydramine HCl) 25 mg₱ 4
  • Hietazyn (Diphenhydramine HCl) 50 mg₱ 10

Description:

Diphenhydramine is a first-generation antihistamine with notable anticholinergic and sedative properties. It rapidly blocks histamine receptors to stop allergic reactions but frequently causes drowsiness as a side effect.

Uses:

  • Treatment of acute allergic reactions and hives
  • Relief of severe itching
  • Short-term management of insomnia (as a sleep aid)
  • Relief of minor cold symptoms
