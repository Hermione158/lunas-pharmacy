Description:

Loperamide is an antimotility agent. It works by binding to gut wall receptors to slow down the transit time of intestinal contents, which allows the body to absorb more water and electrolytes, thereby solidifying the stool.

Uses:

  • Symptomatic control of acute diarrhea
  • Management of traveler's diarrhea
  • Reduction of stool volume in patients with ileostomies
