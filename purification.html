Water Purification
(AquaTab)

Categories: Other Specialty & Oral Care

Price:

Description:

AquaTabs are effervescent water purification tablets that release active chlorine when dissolved. They rapidly destroy harmful microorganisms, including bacteria, viruses, and cysts, present in contaminated water sources.

Uses:

  • Purification of potentially contaminated drinking water
  • Ensuring safe hydration during travel or outdoor activities
  • Emergency water treatment during disasters
