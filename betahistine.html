Description:

Betahistine is a histamine analog that works by improving microcirculation in the inner ear. This reduces the buildup of endolymphatic fluid pressure, thereby restoring balance and reducing vertigo.

Uses:

  • Treatment of Ménière's disease
  • Management of recurrent vertigo
  • Relief of tinnitus (ringing in the ears)
