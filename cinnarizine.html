Cinnarizine
(Moticon, Cinzitab)

Categories: Anti-Vertigo & Motion Sickness

Price:

  • Moticon (Cinnarizine) 25 mg₱ 14
  • Cinzitab (Cinnarizine) 25 mg₱ 8

Description:

Cinnarizine is an antihistamine and calcium channel blocker that affects the smooth muscle cells of blood vessels. It decreases the excitability of the vestibular labyrinth in the inner ear, reducing sensory conflict.

Uses:

  • Prevention and treatment of motion sickness
  • Management of peripheral vascular disease symptoms
  • Treatment of vertigo and vestibular disorders
