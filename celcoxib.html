Celecoxib (Celcoxx, Emicox)

Categories: Pain Relief & Anti-Inflammatory (Analgesic / NSAID)

Price:

  • Emicox (Celecoxib) 200 mg₱ 14
  • Emicox (Celecoxib) 400 mg₱ 40

Description:

Celecoxib is a COX-2 selective nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID). It specifically blocks the COX-2 enzyme responsible for inflammation and pain, while sparing the COX-1 enzyme, making it gentler on the stomach lining compared to traditional NSAIDs.

Uses:

  • Management of osteoarthritis
  • Relief of rheumatoid arthritis symptoms
  • Treatment of acute pain
  • Management of ankylosing spondylitis
