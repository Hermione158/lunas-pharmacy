Description:

Paracetamol is a widely used central analgesic and antipyretic agent. It works by blocking chemical messengers in the brain that tell us we have pain and by affecting the chemical messengers in an area of the brain that regulates body temperature.

Uses:

  • Reduction of fever
  • Relief of mild to moderate headaches
  • Relief of tension headaches
  • Management of mild body aches and joint pain
